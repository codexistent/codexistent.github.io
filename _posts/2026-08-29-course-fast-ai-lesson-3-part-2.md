## Chapter 4 Notes

### Terms
- **Rank:** number of axes or dimensions of tensor, equal to `tensor.ndim` or `len(tensor.shape)`
- **Shape:** size of each axis of tensor, aka. the values in your tensor

Two main ways data scientists measure distance:
- **$$L^1$$ norm/mean absolute difference:** Mean of absolute differences
- **$$L^2$$ norm/RMSE:** Root mean squared error, or mean of squares of differences
