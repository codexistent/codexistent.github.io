#  Another Poem

Woah, I cannot believe my consistency. Post two.

It is currently 11:11 PM, the luckiest time of the day; in August, the luckiest month of the year; and look, now it is 11:12. It is the 12th! Perhaps I am onto something.

Ok, now in order to fulfill my promise of blogging while being time-efficient, here's another random poem I wrote two years ago and thus do not hold accountability for[^1]:
> **Half The Void - Part I**
> Some places/
> Are like ghosts
>
> Like half forgotten dreams/
> Mirages of past/
> the ever-youth of sky/
> Loving what is not known/
> Meaningless
>
> Somewheres I see but/
> Can’t place
Apparently, younger me believed I should start a poem which came in a series of parts. Conveniently, currently with college apps and much ado, there is little time to write original text for the time being. Thus, and conveniently as perhaps a token for you to continue reading, I will be releasing the parts of this poem as the days go past. Do note all of this was written much time ago, when I had not yet known this would be posted on a blog for all the world to see. So be warned and wary of the latter fact.

Here, as well, is a snippet of another poem I wrote. One which as a whole was not fabulous, but when reading over this one line, it maybe wasn't so bad:
> She touches me timid and gently like memories forgetting
