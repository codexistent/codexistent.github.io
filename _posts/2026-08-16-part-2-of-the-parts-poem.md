#  Part II of the Parts Poem

Today I deployed my first AI model! 

Had to write lots to document the process, so I don't have quite as much vigor to write some more, at least for the day being.  So instead, here's part 2 of the parts poem, for what it's worth. As a disclaimer I put up every time I release such poetry, this was written over 2 years ago. I may have been much different back then. It's fun to look back at old writing.
> II
> 
> Where am I?
> 
> There are still\
> Places with no bus to go
>
> Perhaps I shall\
> Live and die
> 
> And know only the\
> Simplest things
> 
> Perhaps the fish\
> Are only fish
> 
> And I\
> Am only human
> 
> Under the blanket\
> Of surfaces and\
> Boundaries
> 
> Physics and the sciences\
> Has writ
